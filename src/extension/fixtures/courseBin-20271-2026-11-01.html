NAUT-301A: Seamanship and Navigation
An experiential approach to sailing and seafaring, introducing offshore sailing theory and techniques, navigation, and basic oceanography as relevant to seamanship, leadership and communication skills. NOTE: This is the entry-level NAUT course, focusing on an overview of vessel nomenclature and construction, sailing physics, basic navigation, seamanship, and the history of sailing ships. This course also includes two field classes, one at the dock and one overnight voyage onboard a classic brigantine tallship. Students will learn first-hand about skills needed to operate a sailing ship at sea and what is like to be crew on a square-rigged sailing vessel. Please consult the course syllabus for complete details. Duplicates Credit in former NAUT 001a.
